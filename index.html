                ? How it works

                This is my sheet music portfolio. Published Scores holds finished arrangements with videos and store links, the Vote page decides what I arrange next, and friends with the passcode get the private vault.

                Settings in the menu has inverted colors, text size and more. This is V0.9 — if you find a bug, tell me through the contact form below or on Instagram.

                Every card is a finished arrangement. Tap a card for details, the play button to hear it, and the store buttons to get the sheet music.

                The search box, the instrument dropdown and the genre chips stack together to narrow things down.

                Want something that is not here yet? Head to the Vote page and suggest it.

                Vote once per song — up if you want it arranged, down if you do not. The board re-sorts live and the bar under each row shows relative support.

                Press play to preview a song. A player bar appears at the bottom of the screen with a draggable progress slider.

                In progress means I am working on it, and Arranged links to the finished score. Suggest new songs with the form below — adding your name is optional.

                Enter the passcode I gave you and the vault unlocks on this device — no account needed. If the code does not work, ask me; it may have changed.

                These are drafts and private arrangements — please keep them between us.

                Buttons on each score: Drive opens the folder with every part, PDF grabs a single file, MuseScore opens the online score, and play starts a recording when one exists.

                The quiz

                500 questions, drawn so nothing repeats in a run: 150 easy, 250 normal, 50 hard, 50 very hard. You start with 3 lives — a wrong answer or a timeout costs one, a correct very hard answer gives one back (max 5). Easy and normal give you 5 seconds; hard and very hard give 10. On a keyboard, keys 1 to 4 answer instantly.

                The pity bar

                Easy answers add 10, normal add 5. Each time the bar climbs another 50, a hard question arrives and knocks it back 50. Fill it to 250 and a very hard question is guaranteed, costing 100. You never get a hard question immediately after a very hard one, and yes — the bar can go negative.

                Easy · Normal · Hard · Very hard

                Leaderboards

                Top 10, ranked by questions answered, then correct answers, then time. Land on it and you can claim it with a name of 3 to 13 characters — keep it clean. The info mark on any entry reveals that player’s difficulty breakdown. The snake on the 404 page has its own board: apples eaten, then fastest time. Swipe, arrows or WASD; apples feed you, sheet music kills you.

                Annoying mode has its own way out. Good luck finding it.
